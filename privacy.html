Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026
// in short
  • We collect what you type into the contact form (name, email, project details) and basic Google Analytics data (which pages you visit).
  • We use it only to respond to you and to understand which pages are useful — never sold, never shared with advertisers.
  • Two services touch your data: Formspree (delivers your form submission to our email) and Google Analytics (site usage stats).
  • Email us anytime to have your data deleted.

What this page is

This is a plain-language summary of what TXL Studio collects on this site and why — not a substitute for legal advice. If you have questions, email help.txlcustomer@gmail.com directly.

What we collect

Two sources of data on this site:

  • The contact form. Name, email, company/business (optional), project type, approximate budget (optional), timeline (optional), and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Google Analytics. Standard usage data — pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type, and referring site. This is aggregate and isn't tied to your name unless you also submit the contact form.

How it's used

Contact form submissions are used only to respond to your inquiry and discuss the project — never sold, never used for marketing without asking first. Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

Who else sees it

  • Formspree processes contact form submissions and forwards them by email. See Formspree's privacy policy for how they handle data in transit.
  • Google Analytics processes usage data per Google's privacy policy.

Nobody else. No ad networks, no data brokers, no resale.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to recognize return visits. You can block these in your browser's privacy settings without affecting anything else on this site.

Your rights

You can ask for any data you've submitted through the contact form to be deleted at any time — email help.txlcustomer@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Check back here periodically.

Questions about this policy: help.txlcustomer@gmail.com
