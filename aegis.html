// what we check

Eight checks, all non-destructive, all with a real fix attached.

No exploitation, no credential attacks, no guessing at what's wrong — every finding ships with the exact header, config line, or setting to change.

01

Security headers

CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Permissions-Policy and more — checked and explained.

02

TLS & certificates

Protocol version, expiry, and chain validity — before a browser warns your visitors for you.

03

Rate limiting

Can login, checkout, or contact forms be hit repeatedly with nothing slowing them down?

04

Exposed files

.env, .git, backup dumps — checks for the files that should never be publicly reachable.

05

Cookies & sessions

Secure and HttpOnly flags, session token hygiene.

06

Brute-force defense

A bounded, dummy-credential probe checks whether login lockout or throttling exists at all.

07

HTTP methods

Flags risky verbs like TRACE or unrestricted PUT/DELETE left open by mistake.

08

Mixed content

Plain-HTTP resources quietly loaded on an HTTPS page.

// how it works

Three steps, and you're not waiting on a consultant's calendar.

01

Scan

We confirm permission first, then run the checks against your live site — a few minutes, nothing invasive.

02

Grade & fix list

An A–F grade plus a prioritized "what to fix" list — each item with the actual config line, not a vague warning.

03

Re-verify

Ship the fixes, we re-scan, you get proof it's resolved — for a client, an investor, or your own peace of mind.

// why TXL Studio built this

We ship the sites. We got tired of shipping them unhardened.

TXL Studio builds websites, dashboards, and AI-powered chatbots for businesses. TXL Aegis grew out of a habit from that work: check what we ship, not just that it works. Now it's available as its own thing — for any site, not only the ones we built.

It's not a penetration-testing toolkit and it doesn't pretend to catch everything. It's the fast, honest first pass every live site should get.

✓
Authorization required, always. Every scan needs confirmed permission before it runs — no exceptions.
✓
Non-destructive by design. Read-only checks and a handful of bounded probes — nothing that can break a live site.
✓
Plain-English output. A grade and a fix list a business owner can understand, not just a developer.
✓
Built alongside real client work. Shaped by shipping actual e-commerce and business sites, not written in the abstract.
