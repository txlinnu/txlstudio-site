Scope of work

Each project's scope is defined in writing (email or the contact form) before work begins. Changes to scope after work starts may affect the price and timeline — I'll flag this before doing extra work, not after.

Payment

50% deposit before work begins, the remaining 50% due before final delivery/handover. Payment methods are arranged directly over email.

Revisions

Each project includes two rounds of revisions during development. Additional revision rounds beyond that are billed separately at an hourly rate agreed on beforehand.

Timeline

Timelines are estimated at the start of each project based on scope. Delays caused by late feedback or missing content/assets on the client's side may extend the timeline accordingly.

Ownership

Full ownership of the final code and design transfers to the client upon receipt of full payment. Until then, TXL Studio retains ownership of the work in progress.

Support after launch

Every project includes 6 months of free bug-fixing support after launch, covering defects in what was delivered. New features or scope requested after launch are quoted and billed separately.

Cancellation & refunds

If a project is cancelled before completion, the deposit is non-refundable — it covers work already started. No further payment is due for work not yet started.

Third-party costs

Domain names, hosting, and any third-party services or licenses required for the project are billed separately unless otherwise agreed in writing.

TXL Studio is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from use of the delivered product.
