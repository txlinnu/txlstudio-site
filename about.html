// who i am

An engineering-led approach to building with AI

TXL Studio was founded to close the gap between concept and execution — delivering functional, production-ready products rather than theoretical exercises. Based in India, my background in cybersecurity informs a security-first approach to development: every product is evaluated not only on whether it works, but on whether it is safe to run.

"I own the architecture, feature scope, and product decisions end-to-end — then use AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT to accelerate implementation."

Every product is rigorously tested before release. This is not a shortcut; it is a modern, efficient development methodology, and one I communicate transparently to every client.

This methodology has delivered a complete e-commerce build for a real client, Base Fragrances, and BFL Infra, a self-directed concept project applying the same approach to conversion-focused business websites. Both are featured in the portfolio below.

I've also built two AI chat products for myself, TXL Cloud and TXL GPT — local-first alternatives to hosted AI chat, both live in production — as a way of staying hands-on with the same AI infrastructure I build for clients.

That security-first background is also why I built TXL Aegis — a security scanner that checks any live site for the same gaps I audit for on every client build. It ships complimentary with anything I build for you, and stands alone for sites I didn't build.
